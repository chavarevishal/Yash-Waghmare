The person behind the camera

Hi, I'm Shreyash — I turned a hobby into a full-time life on the road.

What started as weekend trips with a phone camera turned into a daily habit of packing a bag, hitting record, and figuring out the story as I go. No scripts, no filters — just real roads, real people, and real Bharat.

Today, travel isn't something I do between jobs — it is the job. Every vlog on Unfiltered Shreyash and every post on Shreyash Vlogs is one more page of a journey to see every corner of this country, up close.
